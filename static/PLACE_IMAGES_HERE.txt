Place all site images in this folder. The site expects these filenames:

  favicon.png
  og-image.jpg               (1200x630 social share image)

  BeforeInterior.png   AfterInterior.png
  BeforeExterior.png   AfterExterior.png
  BeforeHeadlight.png  AfterHeadlight.png
  BeforeTire.png       AfterTire.png
  BeforeCarpet.png     AfterCarpet.png
  BeforeEngineBay.png  AfterEngineBay.png

Copy your existing images from your current website here. The page references
them at /static/<filename>, which Flask (and the nginx config) serve from this
folder.
